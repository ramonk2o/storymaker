# Writing the post

Used for the short post's draft, the hooks, the sample passage and the writing after the gate. These are rules the model doesn't follow on its own; they hold for every format, and the voice traits override them when they ask for something else. Facts follow "Nothing invented" in `SKILL.md`.

## The script is the map
- One unit per script line, in order, making that line's claim with its proof.
- A unit that wants to say something its line doesn't is a sign the script needs to change: stop and ask, don't let the post drift.
- Each unit reads on its own: no unit starts mid-sentence or depends on the previous one for its grammar.

## The first line
- It says something: the claim, the number, the scene. No warm-up, no "A thread", no restating the subject.
- It fits before the network's cut with room to spare.
- It promises only what the post delivers.

## Units and claims
- Every claim gets a specific detail from the script or the material: a number, a case, a name, a scene. If there is none, cut the claim or ask; never supply an experience, scene or result the author didn't give you.
- One idea per unit. A unit that needs two ideas is two units, or one idea too many.
- Certainty matches the evidence.
- Carousel slides: a headline and at most a short body; if a slide needs a paragraph, it is two slides or a line for the caption.

## Habits to avoid
These mark a post as written on autopilot:
- "Not X, but Y" reversals.
- Lists of three by reflex.
- A rhetorical question as a hook or transition.
- One sentence per line throughout.
- Emojis, hashtags or "What do you think?" added by reflex.
- A last unit that restates the post or only asks for follows.
- Frequent dashes.

## Format
- Plain text, ready to paste: no Markdown the network won't render (bold, headings, bullet syntax).
- Line breaks follow the voice and the network's habits.
- Links, mentions and hashtags only where the plan puts them, placed as the network's current behavior suggests.

## Length
Each unit within its network's limit, from the research, with a margin. A thread or carousel keeps the unit count of the approved script; if the content needs more or fewer, ask.
