# Stress test

Used in the Message step when the stakes are high. The goal is to press the central idea until it can stand up to the replies: better for the post to take the beating here than in public.

## How to run it
- Use the question-round format from `SKILL.md`; usually one round is enough.
- **Open with the pre-mortem.** "The post is out and it went wrong: people scrolled past, misread it, quoted the first post against the author, or piled on in the replies. Why?" Recommend the two or three most likely reasons for this post, network and reader, each with how the plan answers it.
- **Ground each question in this post.** Find the weak spot in the material (a number with no comparison, one case standing in for a pattern, a claim that reads worse out of context) and recommend how to defend or fix it. A generic question gets a rubber-stamp answer.
- The bank below is a menu, not a script.

Every objection raised is resolved: it changes the central idea or a unit, becomes the objection unit, or is left out on purpose and the user is told so.

## Question bank
- **Central idea:** Could a reasonable person disagree? What does the reader gain by stopping for it? Has it been posted a hundred times, and what does this one add?
- **Out of context:** How does the first unit read if quoted alone, or screenshotted without the rest? Which line could be taken as something the author didn't mean?
- **Evidence:** Where does each number come from? Which is the most fragile? Is a single case presented as a pattern?
- **Author:** Why is this account the one to say it? Is the author ready for the strongest reply, and does the post answer it or leave it for the replies?
- **Brand or employer:** For a brand or a public figure, does anything here commit them to something they haven't approved?
- **Cut:** Which unit is weakest, and what is lost without it?
