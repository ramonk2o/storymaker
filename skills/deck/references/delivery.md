# Speaker notes and likely questions

Used in step 2.2, in the express build, and when delivering. The deck's impact depends on what is said with it, so the skill hands over the words that go with the slides. It doesn't coach how to deliver the talk.

## Speaker notes
For live and hybrid decks, write notes for every slide:
- **Live:** what to say, in the presenter's voice, in a few short sentences — the point of the slide, the one thing to show on the visual, and the transition to the next slide. Not a script to read word for word.
- **Hybrid:** full reading text, so someone who gets the file later understands the slide without the presenter.
- **Moments:** for the hook, the memorable moment and the close, the exact line to say, since those are the ones people repeat.
- **Timing:** mark where the presenter should be at the halfway point, and which slides to drop first if the slot is cut.

## Likely questions
From the stress test, the cold reader and the case's specific stress test, list the questions the audience is most likely to ask, most likely first, each with:
- a short answer the presenter can say;
- the slide or appendix slide that backs it, if any;
- whether it is logical, emotional or practical resistance, when that changes how to answer.

Keep it to the five to eight that matter. An objection the deck chose to leave out appears here with the reason, so the presenter isn't caught off guard.
