# Case: skill

A lesson to make learners better at something that takes judgment and practice: writing a clear email, giving feedback, negotiating, interviewing, presenting data. The goal is that **their next attempt is visibly better than their last one**; knowing the rules is only the start.

## What the learner needs to leave with
- A few criteria for good, concrete enough to judge their own work.
- One attempt of their own, with feedback against those criteria.
- What to practise next, on their own.

## Diagnosis questions
- What does a good result look like, and a typical weak one? Is there a real example of each?
- What do learners do today that the lesson should change?
- How much of the time can be practice with feedback, and who gives it (the teacher, peers, a checklist)?
- What would they practise on: their own real work, or a prepared case?
- How will they keep practising after the lesson?

## How to phrase the objective
The performance + the criteria + the condition.
- Weak: "Learn to give feedback."
- Strong: "Gives a colleague feedback on a real piece of work that names the behaviour, its effect and one change, in under three minutes, without softening it into praise."

## Recommended arcs
- **Model → criteria → attempt → feedback → second attempt** (default): show a strong and a weak example, draw the criteria from them, practise, improve.
- **Attempt first**, for learners who think they already know: they try, compare with a model, then learn the criteria.

## Practice that fits
Compare a strong and a weak example and say why; practise on a prepared case, then on their own work; peer feedback with a checklist; role play with an observer. The second attempt is the evidence.

## Sample module
The first practice with feedback, with its model examples and criteria checklist.

## Pitfalls
- Rules and tips with no practice.
- Model examples so polished that learners can't see the steps.
- Feedback with no criteria ("good job"), or criteria too abstract to check.
- One attempt and no second one.

## Specific stress test
- Could a learner use the criteria to judge their own work alone?
- Is the practice case close enough to their real work?
- What will they practise next week, and how?
