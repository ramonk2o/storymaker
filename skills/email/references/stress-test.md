# Stress test

Used in the Strategy step of the full path. The goal is to press the move until it holds: better for the email to take the beating here than in the reader's inbox.

## How to run it

- Use the question-round format from `SKILL.md`; usually one round is enough.
- **Open with the pre-mortem.** "The email was sent and it went wrong: the reader got angry, didn't act, forwarded it to the wrong person, replied to everyone, or remembers it years later. Why?" Recommend the two or three most likely reasons for this email and these readers, each with how the card answers it.
- **Ground each question in this email.** Find the weak spot in the situation (a date not yet confirmed, a reader who will feel exposed, a line that reads worse forwarded) and recommend how to fix it. A generic question gets a rubber-stamp answer.
- The bank below is a menu, not a script.

Every objection raised is resolved: it changes the card, becomes a block, or is left out on purpose and the user is told so.

## Question bank

- **Objective:** What exactly should the reader do after reading? Does the email give them everything they need to do it?
- **Reader:** What is the first thing they will feel? What will they reply? Who else will see it?
- **Forwarded:** How does it read forwarded to someone outside the thread, or screenshotted? Which line would the user regret?
- **Facts and commitments:** Is every date, number and promise confirmed? Does the email commit the user to something they can't deliver, or admit something they didn't mean to?
- **Power:** Does it corner someone in front of others? Does it leave the reader a way to say yes?
- **Channel:** Should part of this be a call, a word with someone first, or a separate email?
- **Timing:** When will it be read (a Friday night, right before a meeting, in the middle of a crisis)?
- **Cut:** Which block is defensive, and what is lost without it?
