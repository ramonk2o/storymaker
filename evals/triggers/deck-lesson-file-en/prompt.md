# Lesson: reading a P&L in 30 minutes
Objective: given a one-page P&L, learners say whether the business made money and why, on their own.
The one thing: profit is what's left after every cost, not revenue minus the obvious ones.

## M1. Revenue is not profit
Example: a café with $40k monthly sales and $43k in costs.
Mistake: reading the top line as the result.
Try it: find the net result on the sample P&L.

## M2. Fixed and variable costs behave differently
Example: rent stays at $6k whether 500 or 900 customers come in.
Mistake: cutting a fixed cost and expecting it to scale with sales.
Try it: sort the sample costs into fixed and variable.
